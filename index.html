🔍 Why Did Top Models "Fail" Urgency*?

Analysis of the 12 urgency questions shows that 14 out of 17 runs failed on the exact same two support cases where JEV Reference ground truth was highly controversial.

Controversial Case 1 (14/17 Models Disagreed) support-001
"I was charged twice for my subscription this month. Please refund the duplicate."
JEV Ground Truth:
urgent = False (0.0)
Open Models Consensus:
urgent = True (85%–100% prob)

JEV's prompt strictly defines urgency as only "active outage, blocked critical work, or immediate deadline". However, modern instruct models prioritize billing double-charges as urgent customer support.

Controversial Case 2 (14/17 Models Disagreed) support-009
"Our card was declined. Where can I change the payment method?"
JEV Ground Truth:
urgent = False (0.0)
Open Models Consensus:
urgent = True (65%–98% prob)

Card declines mean imminent service cut-off. 14 of 17 model runs flagged this as urgent, causing synthetic "accuracy penalties" on models that were behaving correctly in human customer service context.

📖 Benchmark Metrics Glossary & Measurement Legend

Authoritative mathematical definitions, calibration standards, and interpretation guidelines for all evaluation measures.

Standard: JEV Reference v1.13 Ground Truth
Scoring Precision Lower is Better
Score MAE (Mean Absolute Error)

Measures the average numerical discrepancy between the model's continuous prediction and ground truth on ordered numerical rating decisions (e.g. churn severity, sentiment intensity, priority scores on 1–5 scale).

MAE = (1/N) * Σ |yi - ŷi|
0.000 (Perfect Calibration): Gemma 4 26B (flawless score match)
< 0.100 (Enterprise Grade): Spark X 4B (0.045), Qwen 35B (0.051), Qwen 27B (0.058), KwaiPilot (0.061)
0.100 – 0.250 (Good): Qwen 3.5 9B (0.146), Gemma E4B (0.162), Ornith 35B (0.167)
> 0.300 (Calibration Drift): Qwen3 2507 (0.316), MiniCPM 2.6B (0.405), Laya (0.671)
Core Robustness Higher is Better
Clean Acc (Excl. Urgency*)

Categorical accuracy across the 44 core System-One routing, topic classification, churn risk, and claim verification decisions, completely excluding the 12 controversial support urgency items.

Clean Acc = (Correct in 44 Non-Urgent Qs / 44) * 100%
100.0% Perfection: Achieved by 7 open models (Qwen 9B, Gemma 26B, KwaiPilot, Ornith 35B, Qwen 35B, Qwen 27B, Qwen Flash)

Overall Fidelity Higher is Better
Full Suite Accuracy (56 Qs)

Raw overall accuracy evaluated over all 36 test cases and 56 questions in the authentic JEV Core suite, including all multi-choice, scoring, and the strict urgency edge cases.

Full Acc = (Total Correct Answers / 56) * 100%

User-Facing Speed Lower is Better
p50 Latency (Median Duration)

The 50th percentile inference duration in milliseconds. Exactly half of the decisions are returned faster than this threshold. Unlike mean average, p50 is immune to cold-start outliers.

p50 = Median(latency1, latency2, ..., latencyN)
< 100 ms (Microservice Speed): Laya (53.7 ms), MiniCPM 1B (99.2 ms)
100 – 700 ms (Realtime Interactive): MiniCPM 2.6B (140 ms), Qwen3 2507 (150 ms), Qwen 9B (646 ms)
1.0 – 6.0 s (Heavyweight MoE): Gemma 26B (1.2s), Qwen 35B (4.0s), KwaiPilot (5.2s), Ornith (5.8s)
JEV Baseline: 744.0 ms (Center reference point)
Pipeline Throughput Higher is Better
RPS (Requests Per Second)

Effective decision throughput measured on local hardware under concurrent gateway query load (--parallel 2). Dictates how many simultaneous user interactions the engine can sustain.

RPS = Total Completed Queries / Total Elapsed Time (sec)
> 15 RPS: Laya (16.9 RPS) — single process handles high-volume traffic
5 – 10 RPS: MiniCPM 1B (9.8 RPS), MiniCPM 2.6B (7.0 RPS), Qwen3 2507 (6.7 RPS)

Hardware Sizing Lower is Better
VRAM Requirement (GB)

Total dedicated GPU Video Memory required to load the model weights and allocate the 4096-token KV cache context under LM Studio / vLLM. Dictates hosting hardware costs.

VRAM = Model Weights (GB) + KV Cache (GB) + CUDA Overhead
< 6 GB (Edge / Laptop): MiniCPM 1B (1.2GB), MiniCPM 2.6B (2.7GB), Qwen3 2507 (4.3GB), Bonsai 27B (4.7GB), Laya (4.8GB), Qwen 4B (5.8GB)

Cost-to-Value Index Higher is Better
Efficiency ROI Score

Composite index calculating decision accuracy per unit of computing resources consumed. Heavily rewards models that achieve near-100% accuracy with sub-second speed and low VRAM footprint.

ROI = Accuracy (%) / ( Latency (sec) × VRAM (GB) )
